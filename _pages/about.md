## My work and research

I'm a computer scientist specializing in natural language processing, with just enough knowledge in linguistics and statistics to stir things up. My research focuses on large language models, particularly their logical reasoning capabilities. I believe that enhancing the logical coherence of these models will make them more reliable and aligned with human understanding.
My work combines formal linguistics with language models, exploring how formal methods can improve their explainability and interpretability.

I am particularly interested in applying my work on language models to develop language assistants that collaborate with humans to achieve specific goals. My aim is to make these models highly responsive to natural, free-flowing dialogue and ensure they act as reliable assistants. They should avoid "hallucinating" responses and maintain a grounded understanding of their context and surroundings.

Most of my published work has focused on the limitations of language models and their behavior with texts containing logical operators like NOT and EVERY. I have also demonstrated that language models can be highly inconsistent with predicate-argument structures and have a tendency to hallucinate, raising significant questions about the authenticity of their responses.
